About NewsHub Africa

Your trusted source for comprehensive business, technology, agriculture, mining, and energy news across the African continent.

Our Story

NewsHub Africa was founded by Ashley Jordan Chihiya, a Zimbabwean entrepreneur based in Berlin, Germany. Inspired by a deep connection to the continent and a belief that Africa's stories deserve a world-class platform, Ashley set out to build a news aggregator that covers all 54 nations — from the bustling tech hubs of Lagos and Nairobi to the mining corridors of the DRC and the agricultural heartlands of the Sahel.

Our mission is simple: to deliver timely, accurate, and insightful news coverage that empowers professionals, investors, policymakers, and everyday readers to understand the forces shaping Africa's economic and technological future. Our team of dedicated journalists and analysts brings you stories that matter — stories that connect the dots between local developments and global trends.

What We Cover

  • Business: Corporate developments, M&A activity, startup funding, and macroeconomic policy across all 54 African nations.
  • Technology: From fintech breakthroughs to AI research centers, we track the innovations transforming African industries.
  • Agriculture: Commodity markets, climate-smart farming, agtech investments, and food security initiatives.
  • Mining: Critical minerals, extraction technologies, regulatory changes, and sustainability practices.
  • Energy: Renewables, oil & gas, power infrastructure, and the green hydrogen revolution sweeping the continent.

Our Reach

With over 250,000 subscribers across 54 countries, NewsHub Africa has grown into one of the most trusted independent news platforms on the continent. Our daily newsletter reaches C-suite executives, government officials, investors, and entrepreneurs who rely on our reporting to stay ahead of the curve.

We partner with leading African media houses, research institutions, and industry associations to ensure our coverage is both comprehensive and culturally grounded.
