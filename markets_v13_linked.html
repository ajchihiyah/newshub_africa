The Morning Call

Markets opened mixed across Africa this morning. The NGX is up 0.8% following a weaker-than-expected US jobs report, which has boosted global risk appetite. Oil prices climbed 1.2% overnight, supporting energy stocks on the JSE. The Naira strengthened marginally against the dollar in the parallel market. Investors are positioning ahead of Nigeria's MPC meeting next Tuesday.

— NewsHub Africa Markets Desk, 23 July 2026
