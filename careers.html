Careers at NewsHub Africa

Join a team that's shaping the future of African journalism. We're always looking for passionate storytellers, analysts, and technologists.

Why NewsHub Africa?

At NewsHub Africa, we believe great journalism drives great change. Our team spans the continent — from Lagos to Nairobi, Accra to Johannesburg — united by a shared mission to tell Africa's most important stories with depth, speed, and integrity.
